[The Parallel Corpus of Translations of The Tale of Igor's Campaign](https://nevmenandr.net/slovo/) is an electronic tool for the comparative study of texts. The corpus brings together dozens of translations of the Old Russian monument into modern Russian — from the translation of the First Edition and the classic versions by D. S. Likhachev, O. V. Tworogov, R. O. Jakobson, V. A. Zhukovsky, K. D. Balmont, N. A. Zabolotsky and E. A. Yevtushenko to the poetic and prose renderings by A. Yu. Chernov — as well as numerous translations into other languages (Belarusian, Ukrainian, Polish, etc.). The corpus allows the original and the translations to be aligned and compared line by line, which makes it useful for philological research, for teaching Old Russian literature, and for translation studies. The project has been online since 2006; if you use the corpus in scholarly work, the authors ask you to cite the publication by B. V. Orekhov (2009).
