[Commentarii Academiae Scientiarum Imperialis Petropolitanae: Editio Digitalis](https://github.com/nevmenandr/commentarii) is a digital scholarly edition of the *Commentarii Academiae Scientiarum Imperialis Petropolitanae*, the journal published by the Imperial St. Petersburg Academy of Sciences from 1728 onwards, which carried articles on mathematics, physics, astronomy, anatomy, natural history, history and related fields. The edition includes only the Latin texts; articles in other languages (German, French, etc.) are not included. The texts have been transcribed, corrected from OCR errors, encoded in TEI P5 and supplied with metadata suitable for machine processing and scholarly citation. Each volume is linked to its source facsimile (the scans come from the Internet Archive and the British Museum (Natural History), London), and variants and disputed passages are documented in `<note>` elements. Each document is a standalone `<TEI>` file with its own `<teiHeader>`, the volume containers link the documents via XInclude, and the files are encoded in UTF-8. The edition is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence, so the materials may be freely used, distributed and adapted with attribution. If you use the edition in scholarly work, the editor asks you to cite it (Orekhov, Boris. *Commentarii Academiae Scientiarum Imperialis Petropolitanae: Editio Digitalis*. 2026).
