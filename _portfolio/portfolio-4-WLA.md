[The Anthology of World Literature](https://nevmenandr.github.io/WLA/) is a digital anthology that brings together Russian translations of significant monuments of world literature from the earliest times to the New Age. The selection of texts is based on the multi-volume academic [*History of World Literature*](https://feb-web.ru/feb/ivl/) (IVL): the project is organised chronologically along the volumes of the IVL, from the Ancient World and the Middle Ages through the Renaissance, the 17th, 18th and 19th centuries to the turn of the 20th century, and further subdivided by region and literary tradition. Each work is supplied with a historical and literary context and published with an indication of its primary source, and the texts, period and region characteristics, and tables of contents are all connected through a system of internal links. All the translations into Russian were produced with modern artificial-intelligence technologies, which avoids the legal restrictions associated with copyright and makes the texts available to a wide readership; the project is built in Obsidian as a collection of linked Markdown notes with unified metadata. It is addressed to anyone interested in the history of world literature — philology students, teachers, researchers, and general readers who want to encounter the primary sources in their historical context.
