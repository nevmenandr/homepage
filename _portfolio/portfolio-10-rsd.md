[The Russian Stylometric Dataset](https://github.com/nevmenandr/RSD) is a repository of Russian-language texts prepared for stylometric experiments and classroom use. All the texts are in the public domain and are grouped by period, genre and size, with file names arranged so that they can be processed directly with the `stylo` package for R; the collection is suitable for testing hypotheses, evaluating automatic text-classification tools, and organising classes on computational text analysis. The dataset comprises 322 documents and about 16 million words in total (average document length ~49,787 words) by 94 authors, spread over 25 subcorpora and the years 1763–1932. It is deliberately structured to keep works and authors of different periods apart: the corpora cover fiction of the 18th century, the first and second halves of the 19th century and the turn of the 20th century (including a small five-author set and a larger ten-author set for demonstration and testing); author vs. pseudonym (A. P. Chekhov and A. Chekhonte); texts contrasted by the author's gender, age and generation; journalism of the second half of the 19th century; scholarly texts across several disciplines (history, theology, psychophysiology, philosophy); texts by genre and by literary movement (Romanticism vs. Realism, Westernisers vs. Slavophiles); verse vs. prose and poetic metres; characters' speech in prose and drama (Tolstoy's *War and Peace*, Gogol's *The Government Inspector*, Ostrovsky's plays); and generated vs. natural texts (verse produced by char-based LSTM models and prose produced by Transformer-based models). The texts are plain `.txt` files in UTF-8, with file names following the `stylo` convention (`author_title.txt`) and each set accompanied by a `README.md` explaining the encoded names; the repository also provides scripts for dataset statistics and consistency tests. The texts are in the public domain under Russian law, while the metadata, dataset structure and auxiliary scripts are distributed under the GPL-3.0 licence. If you use the dataset in scholarly work, please cite it (Orekhov, Boris. *Russian Stylometric Dataset*, version 1.0, Zenodo, 2026, DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20701309).
