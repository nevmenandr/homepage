accentual-syllabic-verse-in-russian-prose
======

A dataset devoted to accentual-syllabic verse in 19th-century Russian prose has been published in a research repository. The author, Boris Orekhov, presents a collection of 462 literary prose texts in which the stress system has been marked up, making it possible to identify fragments that read as verse.

The dataset is based on texts from the Corpus of 19th-Century Narrative Prose (Sobchuk, Lekarevich, 2020). Stress placement and the search for metrical fragments were performed automatically using a Python package. The principles for identifying accidental meters are described in Orekhov's article "Accidental Meters in 19th-Century Russian Prose."

The structure of the dataset includes two folders: `accented_texts` — the source texts with stresses marked (the sign ' after the stressed vowel) and `meters` — TSV tables with the identified metrical fragments. Each row of the table contains the name of the meter, the text of the fragment, and its start and end positions. The notation used is: Я — iamb, Х — trochee, Д — dactyl, Аф — amphibrach, Ан — anapest, with an indication of the number of feet.

The study presented at the conference "Long and Short Meter: Structures, Genres, Dictionaries" (December 2023) analyzes the patterns behind the appearance of accidental meters. One of the key results was the absence of a statistically significant connection between the metricality of prose and the direct speech of characters. Averaged data by decade reveal a curious effect: before the 1870s the values of metricality grow, in the 1880s they fall sharply, and in the 1900s they again show a tendency to grow.
